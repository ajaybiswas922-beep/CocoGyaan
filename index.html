GST 2.0: Naye Rate Slabs

22 September 2025 se GST mein bada badlav — ab mainly 2 slabs.
RateKuch Examples
0%Fresh doodh, paneer, roti, health & life insurance
5%Soap, shampoo, ghee, namkeen, bicycle
18%Small cars, AC, TV, cement, zyada tar services
40%Pan masala, sugary drinks, luxury cars
Tip: Rate badalne par billing software mein HSN-wise rate update karein.
Part 1 · GST — Page 14

Input Tax Credit (ITC)

Jo GST purchase par diya, use sale ke GST mein se minus karo.
Example: Ramesh Traders (18% GST)
Purchase ₹1,00,000 par GST₹18,000 (ITC)
Sale ₹1,50,000 par GST₹27,000
Output tax − ITC₹27,000 − ₹18,000
Cash mein pay karna hai₹9,000
Formula: Net GST payable = Output GST − Eligible ITC
